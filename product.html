Calshe-Glow

Composition

Calcium + Vitamin D3 + Methylcobalamin + L-Methylfolate + Pyridoxal-5-Phosphate Tablets

Clinical Overview

Calshe-Glow is a nutritional supplement for pregnancy containing calcium, vitamin D3, methylcobalamin, L-methylfolate, and pyridoxal-5-phosphate.

Action

Supports maternal bone health, calcium absorption, fetal neural development, and red blood cell formation; helps meet increased nutritional requirements during pregnancy.
